# Contradictions

Register of conflicts between sources. Every contradiction is also flagged inline on the
page where it matters (see `CLAUDE.md` §3) — this page is the central view.

A contradiction is not a defect to be smoothed away. Two sources disagreeing is
information: about the sources, about how the subject changed over time, or about where
the real uncertainty lies. Record it; resolve it only when there is a reason to.

**Status:** `open` — unresolved · `resolved` — one claim now preferred, with a reason ·
`superseded` — the older claim was correct at the time but no longer holds.
