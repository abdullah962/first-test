# Contradictions

Two kinds of conflict, one register:

- **Source conflicts** — two sources disagree about the same fact.
- **Changes of position** — you thought one thing, later you thought another. Not an error
  to be cleaned up. This is the most valuable data in the wiki: a record of how a mind
  actually moved, with dates, which is exactly what memory does not preserve.

Every entry here is also flagged inline on the page where it matters, as a
`> [!warning]` callout (`CLAUDE.md` §3).

Do not resolve a conflict just to make the wiki tidy. Two sources disagreeing is
information; so is changing your mind twice and landing back where you started.

**Kind:** `conflict` — sources disagree · `changed` — position moved over time.
**Status:** `open` — unresolved · `resolved` — one side now preferred, with a reason ·
`superseded` — the older claim was true at the time and no longer is.
